# Bhag 11 — Pushp Vatika mein Ram aur Sita — plan

## Hook
"मिथिला के बगीचे में राम और सीता की पहली नज़र मिली!" (11 words).

## Shot List
1. Morning Vatika: Ram aur Sita ek-dusre ko dekhte hue (n1 hook).
2. Morning Vatika: Vishwamitra, Ram, aur Lakshman Mithila aate hue (n2).
3. Morning Vatika: Mata Sita puja ke liye Vatika mein aati hain (n3).
4. Morning Vatika: Ram Lakshman se Sita ki sundarta ki baat karte hue (ram1).
5. Morning Vatika: Ram aur Sita ek-dusre ko dekhte hain, prem jaagta hai (n4).
6. Morning Vatika: Sita Mata Gauri se prarthana karti hain (si1).
7. Morning Vatika: Ram aur Sita aage ke liye pranam karte hue, agle bhaag ka sawaal (n5).
