# Poori kahani ke kirdaar (Bhag 10 se)
Niyam: roop banane se pehle tay karo, phir har scene mein wahi (chehra, kapde, rang, chaal, bolne ka dhang, awaaz). Roop apna banao (kahani aur parampara se); serial ke actors ke chehre ya costume ki nakal nahi. Naye devta/kirdaar ka roop pehle granth ke varnan aur parampara se dhoondh kar tay karo. Pehle se tay roop (NIYAM.md section 4) nahi badlenge. Ye list shuruaati hai: kisi bhaag ke facts mein aur koi ho toh jodo.

- Ayodhya: Dasharatha, Kaushalya, Kaikeyi, Sumitra, Ram, Bharat, Lakshman, Shatrughna, Vasishtha, Manthara, darbari, nagarvasi, sainik
- Mithila: Janak, Sita, Urmila, Mandavi, Shrutakirti, swayamvar mein aaye raja
- Rishi aur raaste ke log: Vishwamitra, Gautam, Ahalya, Parashuram, Shravan Kumar, Nishadraj Guha, Bharadwaj, Valmiki, Atri, Anasuya, Agastya, Shabari
- Van aur Lanka (pehla hissa): Jatayu, Shurpanakha, Khar, Dushan, Maricha (aur sone ka hiran roop), Ravan, Kabandh
- Vanar aur bhaalu: Hanuman, Sugriv, Bali, Angad, Jambavan, vanar sena
- Lanka aur yuddh: Vibhishan, Mandodari, Akshay Kumar, Indrajit, Kumbhakaran, Atikaya, Devantak, Narantak, Trishira, Sushen Vaidya, rakshas sena
- Devta aur doot: Garud, Indra (aur unka rath)
