## Seekh (AI ne in Shorts se nikali)
* **Shuruaati Hook (Sawaal/Ankda):** Pehli line mein 'Kya aap jaante hain' jaisa sawaal ya 'Pehle 3 second' jaisa ankda use karke turant attention grab karein. *(Example: "क्या आप जानते हैं कि भगवान हनुमान जी का एक पुत्र भी था?")*
* **Word Count Limit:** Title aur pehli line ko hamesha 6 se 10 shabdon ke andar chhota, crisp aur direct rakhein. *(Example: "What Happened to Mandodari After Ravan Died?")*
* **Raaz aur Hidden Facts:** 'Didn't Tell You', 'Dark Scene', ya 'Delete' jaise shabdon ka istemaal karke raaz kholne ka hook banayein. *(Example: "Mandodari: What the Ramayana Movie Didn't Tell You!")*
* **High-performing Keywords:** Content mein authority dikhane ke liye 'Valmiki Ramayana', 'Original', 'Actually', aur 'VS' jaise shabd baar-baar use karein. *(Example: "Sita Haran: What Does Valmiki Actually Say?")*
* **Chaunkane Wali Baat:** Shuruaat kisi aisi unsuni ya chaunkane wali baat se karein jo viewer ko poora video dekhne par majboor kar de. *(Example: "Did you know that Hanumanji also wrote Ramayan?")*
