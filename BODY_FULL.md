## 1. Body ki bunaavat aur naap

**Head-unit system:** sar ki oonchai = 1 unit. Poori body ko is unit se naapte hain.
- Average bada aadmi ~7.5 heads lamba hota hai. Kala/drawing mein 8 heads le lete hain (7.5 mein pair chhote lagte hain).
- Sar + dhad milke 4 unit, pair (jaanghon ki haddi ke upar se zameen tak) 4 unit; beech mein aadha unit overlap hota hai.
- Kandhe ki chaudai ~2 heads, kulhe ~1.5 heads, kamar ~1 head. Kamar (waist) collar-bone se ~1.5 head neeche. Haath (kaanch se beech wali ungli ke sire tak) ~3 heads.
- Aurat aadmi se lagbhag aadha head chhoti hoti hai; kulhe chaude aur kandhe patle; aakaar zyada gol. Aadmi mein kandhe chaude, kulhe patle, aakaar kone-daar.

**Umar ke hisaab se (heads mein oonchai):**
| Umar | Heads |
|---|---|
| Naya paida | 4 |
| 1 saal ka bachcha | ~4 |
| Shishu | 5 |
| Chhota bachcha | 5.5 |
| 5 saal | 5 se 6 |
| Bachcha | 6 |
| Kishor (teen) | 6.5 se 7 |
| Jawaan | 7.5 |
| Bada | 7.5 se 8 |
| Boodha | ~7 (jhukne se) |

- Bachchon mein sar badhi hui, maatha aur sar ka upar ka hissa chehre ke hisse se bahut bada; rekhayen narm aur gol.
- Bade hote hote rekhayen kone-daar (plane) ban jaati hain, khaaskar aadmi aur boodhon mein.
- Har insaan alag hota hai: sabko ek hi saanche mein banaoge to nirjeev aur ek jaise lagenge.

**[mera niyam]** Ramayan mein saare insaan ek hi size ke rakho (aapka rule), par chehre/body ke chhote farq do: bade-boodhe (Vasishtha, Dasharatha) thode jhuke aur chaude; Ram-Lakshman patle aur seedhe; bachche ho to sar bada.

**[mera niyam]** Engine mein koi joint in hadon se bahar nahi mudna chahiye. Kohni peeche ki taraf nahi mudti. Ghutna aage ki taraf hi mudta hai.

## 3. Chalna (gait)

- Ek chakkar (cycle): 60% pair zameen par (stance), 40% hawa mein (swing). Beech beech mein dono pair zameen par hote hain (double support).
- Stance ke hisse: heel strike, foot flat, mid-stance, heel off, toe off. Swing ke hisse: shuru, beech, aakhir.
- Animation ke 4 pose: contact, down (sabse neeche, wazan aage wale pair par), passing, up (sabse upar, peeche wala pair ungliyon par dhakelta hai).
- Kamar aur kandhe ulte ghoomte hain; haath balance ke liye pair ke ulte hilte hain.
- Samne se dekho: hawa wala pair kamar ko centre se bahar kheenchta hai, isse kandhe ulte ghoomte hain. Wazan ek pair se doosre par jaata hai (S jaisa raasta).
- Pehle kamar chalti hai, kandhe uske baad. Sar stable rehta hai.
- Body ka upar-neeche halka hona chahiye (zyada bounce nahi). Torso ya kandhe zyada ghumaoge to bura lagta hai.
- Chalna shuru karne se pehle wazan thoda neeche jaata hai (anticipation).

**Umar ke hisaab se chaal:**
- **Chhota bachcha:** chaudi base (paon door-door), chhote kadam, tez nahi, heel strike nahi, ghutna kam mudta hai, haath kam hilte hain. Haath ka swing 2.5-3 saal mein shuru, poora paripakv 5-6 saal mein; 7 saal tak adult jaisa.
- **Bada:** upar wala normal chalna.
- **Boodha:** sabse badi baat dheemi chaal; chhote kadam, kuch mein chaudi base. Par 60-70 saal ke healthy log 30 saal jaisa bhi chal sakte hain, isliye sab boodhe ek jaise dheeme nahi.

**[mera niyam]** Character ke umar aur mizaaj ke hisaab se kadam ki lambai, tez/dheema, jhukaav alag rakho.

## 4. Daudna

- Daudne mein dono pair ek saath hawa mein chhoot jaate hain (flight phase). Chalne mein hamesha ek pair zameen par.
- Body aage ki taraf jhuki rehti hai; jitna tez, utni zyada. Seedhi body wali daud treadmill jaisi lagti hai.
- Landing pair body ke neeche aur zyada mudta hai (compression gehri).
- Haath ka swing sabse zyada tab jab dono pair hawa mein hon (chalne mein ye contact par hota hai).
- Daud ke key poses: contact, down, push, peak.
- Walk ko tez karne se daud nahi banti; poses hi alag hote hain.

---

## 5. Mudna (palatna)

- Pehle aankhen, phir sar, phir kuch frames baad body.
- Mudne se pehle ulti taraf thoda twist (taiyari).
- Asli mudne mein wazan shift hota hai, kadam cross hota hai, body beech mein ghoomti hai.
- Dhad kamar ke centre ke aas-paas ghoomta hai; sar apni jagah stable rehne ki koshish karta hai.
- Sar ka mudna halka arc banata hai (seedha nahi); mudte waqt sar thoda neeche jaata hai.

---

## 6. Rukna, shuru karna

- Rukne ke liye ek chhota kadam lo aur wazan us pose ke hisaab se baant do.
- Kamar/pelvis pehle ruk jaati hai; seena thoda aage chalta hai phir wapas; kandhe peeche lagte hain; haath torso ke dheema hone ke baad hilte hain; ungliyan sabse aakhir mein.
- Loose cheezein (chadar, pagdi ke sire, aabhushan) character ruk jaane ke baad bhi thodi der hilti hain. Bhaari cheez zyada door tak jaati aur der se rukti hai.
- Shuru karte waqt pehle wazan thoda neeche (anticipation).

---

## 7. Baithna aur uthna

**Uthna (sit-to-stand), 4 hisse:**
1. Flexion momentum: kamar aur trunk aage jhukte hain (kulhe seat se abhi nahi uthe).
2. Momentum transfer: kulhe seat chhodte hain, aage ki gati upar-aage ki gati mein badalti hai.
3. Extension: body poori seedhi hoti hai.
4. Stabilization: sthir ho jaati hai.
- Dhad aage jhukne se body ka bhaar paon ke upar aata hai; isliye insaan seedha upar nahi uthta.
- Wazan dheere dheere shift hota hai; jhatke se nahi.

**[mera niyam]** Baithna isi ka ulta: pehle dhad aage, phir kulhe peeche-neeche, phir seedha. Bade-boodhon mein zyada dheere aur haath ka sahara.

---

## 8. Indian baithne ke tareeke (asan)

- **Sukhasana:** cross-legged, seedhi reedh, haath ghutne par. Bahut aam tareeka.
- **Vajrasana:** ghutne par baithna, kulhe ediyon par, paas-paas ghutne.
- **Padmasana:** har paon doosri jaangh par.
- **Dandasana:** dono pair aage seedhe, haath kulhon ke paas.
- Kneeling (ghutne par baithna) aadar ya samarpan ka sanket bhi hai.
- Kuch pose (jaise Akarna Dhanurasana) dhanush kheenchne ki mudra ki tarah hote hain.

**Pranam / namaste:** hath jode, ungliyan upar, anguthe seene ke paas, halka jhukna (Anjali mudra). Namaskara mein jude haath maathe tak. Khade ya baithe, dono mein ho sakta hai. Natya Shastra ke hisaab se ye aadar, pooja aur swagat ke liye hai.
**Ye nahi mila:** sashtang pranam (poora lette ke pranam) ke saare steps aur darbar mein baithne ka sahi tareeka; inke liye alag se dekhna padega.

## 9. Haath bolte waqt (gesture)

- Gesture bhavna se milna chahiye (utsaah, jaldi, aaram).
- Sirf haath nahi, poori upper body (kandha, peeth) mil kar hilti hai.
- Zor dene wale shabd par sar ki halki hilaaki ya ungli ka ishaara. Awaaz zor de aur body sthir rahe to ulta asar padta hai.
- Pehle se tay kiye gesture banaawati lag sakte hain; kirdar ke hisaab se rakho.
- Bolte waqt zyada ghoomna ya baar-baar ek hi gesture dohrana dhyaan bhatkata hai.

---

## 10. Haath ki bunaavat

- Anguthe ko chhodkar har ungli mein 3 haddiyan (phalanges); anguthe mein 2.
- Palm ki lambai aur ungliyon ki lambai ~1:1. Beech ki ungli sabse lambi.
- Aaram se khule haath mein ungliyan halki gol (arc), seedhi nahi; thodi si fan jaisi khulti hain. Mutthi mein bhi poora haath ek gol shape banata hai.
- Anguthe ka aadhar kalai ke paas bahut neeche hota hai aur wo sabse zyada azaadi se hilta hai.
- Ungliyon ke jodon par haddi ubhri hoti hai; baaki jagah mote gaddi.
- Zor dikhane ke liye joints zyada mudo.

---

## 11. Aankhen

- Palak jhapakna: lagbhag har 2 second mein ek baar; ek jhapak ~200 ms ki. Asli jhapak symmetric nahi hoti: band hone mein tez, khulne mein dheere.
- Aankhon ki hilaaki (saccade) ke saath jhapak aati hai; bade gaze shift ke baad ~20% sambhavna.
- Baatcheet mein ~50% samay aankhon se sampark; baat shuru hote waqt zyada, ant mein kam. Sunne wala zyada dekhta hai.
- Aankhen hamesha sar se pehle chalti hain. Poori nazar kabhi bhi ek hi jagah jami nahi rehti; chhoti chhoti hilaaki hoti hain.
- Jhapak ka rate kirdar ki dosti aur buddhi ki chhavi par asar daalta hai.

## 12. Chehre ke bhav

FACS (action units) ke hisaab se:
| Bhav | Chehre mein kya hota hai |
|---|---|
| Khushi | gaal upar, honth ke kone upar, aankhon ke kone mein jhurriyan (asli muskaan mein aankh bhi shamil) |
| Dukh | bhawen andar se upar, bhawen paas, honth ke kone neeche |
| Hairat | bhawen upar, aankhen badi, jabda khulta hai |
| Dar | bhawen upar aur paas, aankhen badi, palak tanaav, honth khinche |
| Gussa | bhawen neeche aur paas, aankhen badi, palak kasi, honth kasi |
| Ghrina | naak sikudti, bhawen neeche, upar ka honth upar |
| Tiraskar | ek taraf ka honth kasna |

- Ye bhav alag-alag culture, halat aur insaan mein badal sakte hain; ek hi saanche mein na daalo.
- **Navras (Bharatiya):** shringar, krodh, jugupsa, utsaah (veer), hasya, shok (karun), adbhut, bhay, shaant. Ye aapka pehle se tay navras rang-niyam hai.
- Muskaan ya bolne se pehle chhoti taiyari (honth bhinchna) aur sar mudne se pehle palak jhapakna anticipation hote hain.

## 13. Bolna (lip-sync)

- Awaaz ke har swar-vyanjan (phoneme) ke liye ek mouth shape (viseme). Kai awaazon ka ek hi shape ho sakta hai (jaise f aur v).
- Naya shape tab aata hai jab purana jaane lagta hai; beech mein 100 ms ya zyada dono active hote hain.
- Mouth shape awaaz se thoda pehle shuru hona chahiye.
- Swar: "a" jabda bahut khula; "e" muh chauda, aadha khula; "i" honth khinche, jabda upar; "o" honth gol, aadha khula; "u" honth gol aur thode aage.
- Bolte waqt sar aur bhawen bhi hilti hain, sirf honth nahi.

---

## 14. Saans aur khade rehte waqt (idle)

- Kabhi bhi bilkul sthir mat rakho: saans, wazan ka shift, halki hilaaki zaroori hain.
- Saans: seena aur kandhe upar-neeche; andar lena bahar chhodne se dheema; alag-alag hisse thodi der se react karte hain. Chhoti (1-2 cm jitni).
- Wazan kabhi ek pair par, kabhi doosre par; reedh santulan banaye rakhti hai.
- Hilaaki seedhi upar-neeche nahi, halki gol/8 jaisi.
- Kai parat (layers): saans base, halka sar-hilna, kapde/gehne jhoomna.
- Har kirdar ki apni ek lay ho, sab ek saath ek jaisa na hilein.

## 15. Bhavna aur body

- Body ka sanket chehre se zyada bada hota hai; chehra aur body na milein to log body par bharosa karte hain.
- Khushi: phaili hui (expansive) body. Garv: seena phula, kandhe peeche, thodi upar. Sharm: seena dhans, kandhe gol, thodi andar.
- Sar upar = aatmavishwas, garv. Sar neeche = sharm, vinamrata, aadar.
- **Gussa:** poori body aage, mutthi kasi, maspeshiyan tani, tez saans, awaaz ooncha.
- **Dar:** pehle tez achanak chaunk (saans andar, jam jaana), kandhe upar, ang sikodna, peeche jhukna, haath se bachaav, kaanpna.
- **Dukh:** body dhaali, kandhe gire, dheeme, sar late hilta, haath latke; badlaav dheere (30-90 frames) aur andar-andar.
- **Roona:** shaant ro-na (lagatar aansu, aawaz toote, kandhe halke hilte). Sob (control kho dena): saans tootna, kaanpna, jhukna, aankhen bheench kar band, kisi ko ya khud ko pakadna.
- **Thakaan/haar:** wazan zyada dikhe, sab dheere.
- **Raahat:** pehle thoda dhasna phir sambhalna.

## 16. Bhaari cheez uthana

- Bhaari cheez ke liye zyada anticipation, chaudi stance, aur beech ke poses mein zyada samay.
- Ghutne mudte hain (taangein sabse mazboot hain); body wazan ke theek neeche rehti hai.
- Hath aur paon santulan dhoondhne ke liye adjust hote hain; kandhe mehnat dikhate hain.
- Silhouette mein tanaav aur dabaav dikhe.

---

## 17. Dhanush-baan

- Stance: dhanush wala haath target ki taraf, paon kandhe ki chaudai par, wazan barabar.
- Dhanush wala haath + kheenchne wala haath milke T ka aakar banate hain.
- Dhanush kheenchte waqt kheenchne wale haath ke kandhe ki haddi (shoulder blade) reedh ki taraf aati hai.
- Anchor: dori nak ki nok se lagti hai, ungli thodi ke neeche. Dhanush wali kohni neeche aur bahar mudti hai.
- Chhodne ke baad dhanush ke dabaav ke ghatne se body thoda follow-through karti hai. Chhodne se pehle body chhoti taiyari karti hai.
- Dhanush tan ne par bahut upar na uthao (sky drawing galat).

---

## 18. Talwaar, gada aur ladai

- Bhaari hathiyar (gada, kulhadi) mein taiyari aur recovery dheemi.
- Wazan hathiyar ke hisaab se alag: patli talwaar tez, bhaari dono haath se.
- Vaar se pehle kulhe ghoomte, kandhe twist, wazan ek pair par (taiyari); vaar ke baad body aage jaati hai (follow-through) aur follow-through ko thoda zyada rokna wazan dikhata hai.
- Defence: ek bada kadam peeche, ya vaar ko mod dena, phir seedha jawab.
- Har hathiyar ka alag stance aur swing do.
- Asli vazan ke barabar cheez se reference lo.
- **Ye nahi mila:** Bharatiya gada-yuddh aur talwaar ke khaas tareeke; inke liye alag dekhna padega.

---

## 19. Girna aur ghayal hona

- Girna chhota rakho, sambhalne/baithne mein zyada samay do.
- Body ke alag-alag hisse alag samay par pahunchte hain; kuch bhi ek saath nahi.
- Chehra bhi badalta hai: ghabrahat, nirnay, sambhalna.
- Bhaari cheez/vyakti tez girti hai aur dishaa badalne mein zyada samay leti hai.
- Takkar (impact) ke pose ko zyada dikhao, gire ki disha pehle se pata chale.
- Chhoti-chhoti baatein (paon ka phisalna) asliyat badhati hain.

---

## 20. Har movement mein lagne wale niyam

1. Har movement se pehle ek chhoti taiyari (anticipation), khaaskar ruke hue se.
2. Har movement ke baad follow-through; jo cheez dheeli ya door hai wo der se rukti hai.
3. Alag alag hisse alag samay par chalein (kamar pehle, kandhe baad mein, sar aur aankhen alag lay mein).
4. Aankhen pehle, phir sar, phir body.
5. Jitna bhaari, utni dheemi taiyari aur recovery.
6. Chalne/daudne mein pair zameen par phislein nahi.
7. Body kabhi bilkul sthir nahi.
8. Har insaan/umar/mizaaj ka apna andaaz.

---

## Jo abhi nahi mila (aage dekhna hai)
- Sashtang pranam ke poore steps; darbar mein baithne ka tareeka.
- Bharatiya gada-yuddh, talwaar, aur dhanush-yuddh ke khaas tareeke.
- Ghode, rath, naav par baithna/chalna.
- Kapde aur baalon ki physics (dhoti, pagdi, jata).
- Khana khana, pani peena, pooja karna, havan karna jaise rozmarra ke kaam.
- Asli insaan ka reference video se naap (aap clip dein to).
