# Bhag 8 — Sau Putriyon Ka Shrap (Vishwamitra ka Vansh)
Valmiki Bal Kand sarg 1.32-1.34. Pichhla Bhag 6 Siddhashram aur Vaman tha. Ab Vishwamitra Ram-Lakshman ko apna vansh sunate hain.

## Kahani ka dhaancha
- Hook: Vishwamitra ka vansh, 100 putriyon ka raaz.
- Peechhe ki baat: Kushanabh ki 100 kanyayein, unka ghamand.
- Mod: ek rishi ka shrap.
- Raaz: Gadhi ki tapasya, Brahma ji ka vardaan.
- Nateeja: kanyayein rishiyon se vivah, Gadhi ke putra Vishwamitra.
- Aakhri line: Kausheki nadi, agle bhaag ka sawaal + comment.

## Assumptions
- 100 kanyayein dikhane ke liye engine mein max 4 cast hain, isliye ek princess ko group ka representative rakha. Line mein saaf 100 kanyayein hain.
- Brahma ji ko golden divine form (big + gold glow) dikhaya, kyunki Valmiki mein wo prakat hote hain.
- Kausheki nadi ka zikr Vishwamitra ke ashram ke liye, Valmiki ke anusaar.
- Sabhi naye characters insaan hain, unke peeche halo nahi. Brahma ji devta hain, unke paas golden glow hai.
