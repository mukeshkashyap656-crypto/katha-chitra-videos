# Bhag 7 — Siddhashram Yagya Raksha aur Maricha-Subahu Vadh
Valmiki Ramayan Bala Kanda Sarg 30 ke anusaar.

## ASSUMPTIONS & STORY
- Vishwamitra yagya ke 6 din ka maun vrat lete hain.
- Ram aur Lakshman 6 din-rat nirantar dhanush-baan lekar yagya kund ki raksha karte hain.
- 6th din yagya ke samay aakaash mein kaale baadal chhate hain aur Maricha-Subahu rakt varsha karte hain.
- Shri Ram Manavastra se Maricha ko 100 yojan door samudra mein phenk dete hain (bina maare), aur Agneyastra se Subahu ka vadh karte hain.
- Yagya nirvighna sampanna hota hai.

## SHOT LIST (Every shot 2-3s)
1. Hook: Ashram mein Ram aur Lakshman dhanush taane khade hain, rishi yagya kar rahe hain (n1)
2. Vishwamitra maun vrat aur yagya shuru hone ki baat batate hain (vm1)
3. Shri Ram pranam karke guru ko aashvasan dete hain (ram1)
4. 6th din kaale baadal aur laal rakt-varsha hoti hai, Maricha-Subahu aakaash mein dikhte hain (n2)
5. Maricha ahankaar se yagya nasht karne ki chunauti deta hai (mar)
6. Rakshas yagya kund ko apavitra karne badhte hain (n3)
7. Ram Lakshman ko astra chalane ka nirdesh dete hain (ram2)
8. Action: Ram ka Manavastra Maricha par (samudra mein door jatha hai) aur Agneyastra Subahu par (vadh) (n4)
9. Yagya safal hone par Vishwamitra aashirvaad dete hain, agle bhaag ka teaser (n5)
