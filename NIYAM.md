## 4. Characters
- Har character **har scene mein ek jaisa**: wahi chehra, wahi kapde, wahi rang.
- Kisi bhi devta ka roop, rang aur kapde **pehle Valmiki se tay** karo.
- Sab insaan **ek hi size** ke. Devta bade + sunehri chamak. Rakshas bade + kaali-laal chamak.
- Ram, Lakshman, Dasharatha jaise insaanon ke sir ke peechhe **koi chamak/halo nahi**.
- Jagah kahani ke hisaab se (screen ka koi fixed side nahi). Koi character doosre ke upar ya aar-paar nahi.
- Har character ka apna roop aur swabhaav (chehra, sharir, khade hone ka dhang) — do log ek jaise nahi, peechhe wale bhi.
- Chehre ke bhaav (peechhe wale characters ke bhi) kahani ke us pal se mel khayein.
- Gussa, dar, dukh, khushi **cheez banakar nahi** (aag, taare, baadal nahi) — chehre aur sharir se dikhao.

### Tay roop (character bible)
- **Dasharatha:** buzurg raja, chaude kandhe, thoda pet, gol chehra, maathe par lakeerein, sone ka mukut (laal-hare ratn), safed daadhi-moonchh, sone ke kundal, peela vastra, laal tirchha patka, hara kamarband, nange haath par sone ke baajuband, cream dhoti.
- **Vishwamitra:** dubla, thoda lamba, lamba patla chehra, mudi naak, ghani bhaunhein, tez aankhein; gehri bhoori jata ka jooda (rudraksh ki patti), kandhe tak lat; lambi gehri-sleti daadhi; tripund tilak; valkal vastra + mrigchhala; rudraksh mala/baajuband (sona nahi); haath mein kamandal; tap ki chamak.
- **Guru Vasishtha:** patla, thoda jhuka, shaant aankhein, safed jata (rudraksh patti), lambi safed daadhi, tilak, halka geru valkal + mrigchhala, rudraksh, chamak.
- **Shri Ram:** yuva rajkumar, saanvla-neela rang, badi aankhein, daadhi nahi, chhota sone ka mukut, **halo nahi**, peela pitambar laal kinare ke saath, hara uttariya, laal patka, haath mein dhanush (mutthi mein pakda), peeth par tarkash (kandhe ke upar dikhe).
- **Lakshman:** yuva rajkumar, gora rang, chhota sone ka mukut, halo nahi, narangi-laal vastra peele patke/kinare ke saath, safed uttariya, dhanush + tarkash.
- **Maricha:** rakshas, bada, neela rang, seeng, daant, laal aankhein, bikhre kaale baal, kaale kapde laal patka, gehri laal-kaali chamak.
- **Subahu:** rakshas, bada, maroon rang, seeng, daant, laal aankhein, bikhre baal, gehri daadhi, bhaari sharir, kaale kapde narangi patka, gehri chamak.
- **Tataka:** ghane jungle mein bahut badi kaali parchhai, bikhre baal, chamakti laal aankhein.
- **Darbari:** sab bade log, alag-alag roop — ganja buzurg mantri safed daadhi (hara vastra), mote kaali daadhi safed pagdi, yuva patla laal pagdi neela vastra, gol-matol peeli pagdi sleti moonchh (maroon vastra), adhed hari pagdi ghani moonchh.
- **Siddhashram ke yagya rishi:** sleti jata wale bhagwa vastra; kaali jata wale safed vastra (mrigchhala, rudraksh, tilak).
- **Yagya ki aag se nikle divya purush:** insaanon se bade, sunehri kirnein, sone ka mukut, sunehri tvacha, lambe kaale baal, laal vastra, haath mein kheer ka sone ka paatra.

## 6. Camera aur movement (film director ki tarah)
- Har insaan frame mein **ya poora andar ya poora bahar** — aadha kata nahi. Background ki cheezein bhi kinare par aadhi na katein.
- Sir ke upar maathe jitni jagah (mukut/jata upar ke kinare ko na chhuye); aankhein upar ke teesre hisse par; jis taraf character dekh/chal raha ho us taraf zyada jagah.
- Bolne wale par camera zoom. Raftaar scene ke hisaab se (tez scene mein jaldi cut, bhavuk scene mein dheema lamba shot; VIRAL.md section 3); koi khaali second nahi.
- Movement **smooth** (jhatke nahi): chalne mein pair baari-baari uthein (fisalna nahi); khada hona dheere-dheere; mudna/haath uthana beech ke frames se (dhundhla aar-paar nahi).
- Yagya **baith kar** hota hai (aasan par paalthi maar kar, kund ke chaaron taraf).
- Rishi ke aane par raja **kamar se jhuk kar haath jod ke pranam** karta hai; rishi haath utha ke aashirvaad deta hai. Jhukte waqt koi kisi ko chhuye nahi.
