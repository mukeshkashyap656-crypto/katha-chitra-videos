# Bhag 10 — Ahalya ka uddhar (1987 Ramayan serial) — plan, 28 Nov 2026
Source: serial1987 (ep 5). Sirf 2 facts: (1) Mithila raaste Ram ne Ahalya ko mukt kiya; (2) pati rishi Gautam ne shraap diya tha. Koi aur ghatna/vyakti/shraap-ka-roop nahi joda. TAG: रामायण कथा.

## Hook (trigger: chaunkane wala sach)
n1: "ऋषि ने अपनी ही पत्नी को श्राप दिया था!" (9 shabd). Sawaal banner: "आहिल्या को श्राप से किसने मुक्त किया?" (jawab video mein: Ram). "Kyun shraap diya" ka jawab facts mein nahi — isliye na banaya, na gadha.

## Kahani (sirf facts)
- Ahalya udaas (shraap ka dukh); pati Gautam ne shraap diya (narration).
- Vishwamitra + Ram + Lakshman Mithila ja rahe (raasta).
- Ram ke aane se Ahalya ka shraap tूtta (mukt).
- Ahalya ka aabhar, Ram ka aashwasan.
- Aakhri: agle bhag (Mithila/Sita) ka sawaal + comment + subscribe.

## Shot list (7 shot, ~40 sec, har 2-3 sec)
1. (n1) Ahalya akeli, udaas, aansu — ashram, sorrow, chip "श्राप". [hook]
2. (n2) Ahalya baithi, sar jhuka, chinta — ashram, sorrow.
3. (n3) Vishwamitra aage, Ram-Lakshman saath, chalte hue Mithila ki taraf — morning, wide.
4. (n4) Ram ka aagman (haath uthaya), Ahalya ka chehra chamakta (wonder), sunehri roshni — ashram, wonder, chip "श्राप टूटा".
5. (ah1) Ahalya haath jodkar aabhar (joy), Ram shaant muskurate — ashram, joy, two.
6. (ram1) Ram dridhh aashwasan (talk), Ahalya vishwas — ashram, speaker.
7. (n5) Ahalya prasann, haath jode, jaate hue ki taraf dekhti (Mithila ka raasta) — ashram, wonder, chip "अगला भाग".

## Naya roop (parampara se, apna)
Ahalya: rishi-patni, simple udaas saree (muted brick-red), peela blouse/border, tilak, chhote gehne; insaan (na bada, na chamak). Har scene mein wahi roop.

## Maanyataayein (assumptions)
- Ram ke saath Vishwamitra aur Lakshman hain — serial ka chal raha silsila (Mithila ki taraf teeno). Isliye dikhaye; koi nayi ghatna nahi.
- Shraap ka roop (jaise patthar) NAHI dikhaya — facts mein nahi. Sirf udaas Ahalya + Ram ke aane par mukt.
- Gautam sirf narration mein (scene mein nahi, kyunki facts mein mukt-cheen par upsthit nahi).
