# Bhag 11 — Pushp Vatika mein Ram aur Sita — plan
Srot: 1987 TV Ramayan (Episode 6 facts: Pushp Vatika milan aur pehli nazar ka prem).
Post Tareekh: 2026-11-29 shaam 7 baje.

## Hook & Dhaancha
- Hook line (<= 12 shabd): "मिथिला के उपवन में जब पहली बार राम और सीता मिले!"
- QUESTION banner: "पुष्प वाटिका में कौन मिला?"
- Dhaancha: Hook -> Vatika aagaman -> Ram aur Sita ki pehli nazar -> Prem bhaav -> Agla sawaal (Shiv dhanush) + Subscribe callout.

## Characters & Layout
- Ram: Saanvla, chhota mukut, pitambar, dhanush-tarkash.
- Lakshman: Gora, narangi vastra, dhanush.
- Sita (Naya char): Sunder rajkumari, laal saree, sunehra border, maathay par tilak aur aabhushan.
- Subah ke upvan (morning) background.

## Shot List
1. Shot 1 (n1): Ram aur Sita pehli baar ek doosre ko dekh rahe hain.
2. Shot 2 (n2): Ram aur Lakshman vatika mein phool chunte hue.
3. Shot 3 (n3): Sita ji ka vatika mein aagaman.
4. Shot 4 (ram1): Ram Lakshman se Sita ke divya roop ki baat karte hain.
5. Shot 5 (sita1): Sita ji Gauri pujan/prarthana karti hain.
6. Shot 6 (n4): Dono ke beech pavitra prem ka bhaav.
7. Shot 7 (n5): Agle bhag ka sawaal aur subscribe widget callout.
