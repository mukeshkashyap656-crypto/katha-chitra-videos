## Kahani (Valmiki, kuch jodna/badalna nahi)
Sarga 36: Shiva-Uma ko saath rahte sau divya varsh, putra nahi; devta ghabraye; Shiva se tej rokne ko kaha; Shiva ne maana, par jo tej hil chuka tha use dharan karne ko poochha; Shiva ka tej dharti par faila, Agni (Vayu ke saath) usme sama gaye; Uma ne gusse mein devtaon ko shraap diya: apni patniyon se santan nahi paoge.
Sarga 37: devta senapati chahte the, Brahma ke paas gaye; Brahma: Ganga mein Agni ka putra hoga, Uma bhi use apnayengi; Agni ne Shiva ka tej Ganga ko diya; Ganga ne tap na sahne par kaha; Agni: Himalaya ki talhati mein rakh do; sona, chaandi, taamba, loha bane; Krittikaon ne baalak ko paala -> Kartikeya; Ganga ke garbh se gire -> Skanda; devtaon ne senapati banaya.

## Jaanbujhkar chhoda (samay ke liye, kuch badla nahi)
