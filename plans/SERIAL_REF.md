# 1987 Ramayan serial: kahani ka aadhar
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramayan_(1987_TV_series) (padha: 26 Nov 2026). Wikipedia text CC BY-SA ke tahat hai; ye uska chhota saar hai.
Serial ke episode dekhe nahi gaye. Yahan jo nahi likha, use pakka mat maano.

## Hisse (episode number lagbhag)
- Bal Kand (1-10): Ram aur bhaiyon ka janm, Vasishtha se shiksha; Vishwamitra ke saath Ram-Lakshman, Tadaka vadh; Ahalya (pati Gautam ka shraap) ko Ram mukt karte hain; Pushp Vatika mein Ram-Sita; Shiv dhanush todkar Sita se vivah; Lakshman-Urmila, Bharat-Mandavi, Shatrughna-Shrutakirti; dulhanon ki vidaai.
- Ayodhya Kand (11-26): Sita ka Ayodhya aana, Ram uttaradhikari; Manthara, Kaikeyi ke do var; Ram-Sita-Lakshman ka van jaana, Guha ki madad, Chitrakoot; Dasharatha ka dehant; Bharat raajgaddi thukrate hain, Ram se milte hain, paduka lekar Nandigram se raaj.
- Aranya (27 se): Atri-Anasuya, Agastya, Panchvati, Shurpanakha, Khar-Dushan, sone ka hiran, Sita haran, Jatayu.
- Aage: Hanuman aur Sugriv, Bali vadh, Sita ki khoj, Lanka dahan, Vibhishan, setu, yuddh, Ravan vadh, agni pariksha, rajyabhishek (ep 78).

## Episode ke naam (1-26)
1 Birth and childhood of Lord Ram; 2 Ram going to Gurukul; 3 Continuing Gurukul; 4 Vishwamitra comes to Ayodhya and Ram kills Taraka; 5 Ganga, Redemption of Ahilya; 6 Ram meets Sita; 7 Sita's Swayamvara; 8 Swayamvara and Parashuram; 9 Marriage Preparations; 10 Ram and Sita's Marriage; 11 Sita's arrival at Ayodhya; 12 Discussion on Dasharatha's heir; 13 Manthra poisons Kaikeyi's mind; 14 Kaikeyi demands her boons; 15 Preparing for the forest; 16 Leaving Ayodhya; 17 Meeting Nishadraj Guha; 18 Bharadwaja's Ashram; 19 Valmiki's Ashram and Chitrakoot; 20 Dasharath's death and Shravan Kumar; 21 Bharat returns; 22 Bharat rejects to become king; 23 Bharat vows to bring back Ram; 24 Reunion of Ram and Bharat; 25 Bharat returns with the sandals; 26 Sandals installed on the throne.
