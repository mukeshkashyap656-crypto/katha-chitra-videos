# Katha Chitra — Niyam ki Kitaab (Ramayan videos)

**"Is file ke saare niyam maan kar kaam karo."**

---

## 1. Baat karne ka tareeka
- Jawab **Hinglish** mein (Hindi, Roman likhai), aam bolchaal ke shabd — technical ya product ke naam nahi, jaise kisi aam insaan se baat ho rahi ho.
- Koi baat samajh na aaye (jaise raj-darbar kaisa dikhta tha) toh pehle internet par dekho, phir banao.
- Jo maanga gaya ho wo na ho sake, toh khud raaste dekh ke sahi faisla lo aur bata do.
- Kisi ek video ke liye bataya gaya sudhaar **har video** par lagta hai (purane remake aur har naye) — dobara bolna na pade.

## 2. Kahani (sabse zaroori)
- Sirf **Valmiki Ramayan** (Tulsidas nahi). Viral topic ke liye bhi Valmiki se bahar ki kahani nahi.
- Kahani **bilkul waisi** jaisi Valmiki mein hai, episode-dar-episode aage. **Apni taraf se kuch mat jodo, kuch mat badlo.**
- Pehle us bhaag ka sarga padho, phir likho.
- Script **aaj ki bolchaal wali saaf, sahi Hindi** mein (bhaari kitaabi shabd nahi), aur line se line smooth flow.
- Pehli line sabse dilchasp pal ya sawaal ho — recap ya "आपने देखा" nahi. **Chhoti (12 shabd tak), chaunkane wali baat pehle shabdon mein, awaaz 0.3 sec se shuru, pehle shot mein 1 sec ke andar harkat** (24 Nov 2026: 69.8% log swipe kar rahe the, aausat dekhna ~6 sec; poora kaaran aur udaharan VIRAL.md section 1). Video lagbhag 40–60 sec (75 tak theek) — lambai se zyada zaroori pehle 3 sec ka hook.
- Kahani ka dhaancha: hook → peechhe ki baat → mod → raaz → nateeja → bhaavuk aakhri line.
- Aakhir mein agle bhaag ka sawaal + "कमेंट करें: जय श्री राम".
- Lambai lagbhag 40–60 sec (75 tak theek). Koi khaali second nahi.

## 3. Pehle tay karo, phir banao
- Har scene aur visual **pehle kahani ke hisaab se tay karo**, shot list dikhao, **haan milne ke baad hi** banana shuru karo.
- Har shot 2–3 sec, kahani ke hisaab se cut (jaise naam pukara jaaye toh us character ke chehre par cut).
- Scene ke hisaab se: bhaav zaroori ho toh bada close-up, saath dikhana zaroori ho toh sab poore (wide).

## 4. Characters
- Har character **har scene mein ek jaisa**: wahi chehra, wahi kapde, wahi rang.
- Kisi bhi devta ka roop, rang aur kapde **pehle Valmiki se tay** karo.
- Sab insaan **ek hi size** ke. Devta bade + sunehri chamak. Rakshas bade + kaali-laal chamak.
- Ram, Lakshman, Dasharatha jaise insaanon ke sir ke peechhe **koi chamak/halo nahi**.
- Jagah kahani ke hisaab se (screen ka koi fixed side nahi). Koi character doosre ke upar ya aar-paar nahi.
- Har character ka apna roop aur swabhaav (chehra, sharir, khade hone ka dhang) — do log ek jaise nahi, peechhe wale bhi.
- Chehre ke bhaav (peechhe wale characters ke bhi) kahani ke us pal se mel khayein.
- Gussa, dar, dukh, khushi **cheez banakar nahi** (aag, taare, baadal nahi) — chehre aur sharir se dikhao.

### Tay roop (character bible)
- **Dasharatha:** buzurg raja, chaude kandhe, thoda pet, gol chehra, maathe par lakeerein, sone ka mukut (laal-hare ratn), safed daadhi-moonchh, sone ke kundal, peela vastra, laal tirchha patka, hara kamarband, nange haath par sone ke baajuband, cream dhoti.
- **Vishwamitra:** dubla, thoda lamba, lamba patla chehra, mudi naak, ghani bhaunhein, tez aankhein; gehri bhoori jata ka jooda (rudraksh ki patti), kandhe tak lat; lambi gehri-sleti daadhi; tripund tilak; valkal vastra + mrigchhala; rudraksh mala/baajuband (sona nahi); haath mein kamandal; tap ki chamak.
- **Guru Vasishtha:** patla, thoda jhuka, shaant aankhein, safed jata (rudraksh patti), lambi safed daadhi, tilak, halka geru valkal + mrigchhala, rudraksh, chamak.
- **Shri Ram:** yuva rajkumar, saanvla-neela rang, badi aankhein, daadhi nahi, chhota sone ka mukut, **halo nahi**, peela pitambar laal kinare ke saath, hara uttariya, laal patka, haath mein dhanush (mutthi mein pakda), peeth par tarkash (kandhe ke upar dikhe).
- **Lakshman:** yuva rajkumar, gora rang, chhota sone ka mukut, halo nahi, narangi-laal vastra peele patke/kinare ke saath, safed uttariya, dhanush + tarkash.
- **Maricha:** rakshas, bada, neela rang, seeng, daant, laal aankhein, bikhre kaale baal, kaale kapde laal patka, gehri laal-kaali chamak.
- **Subahu:** rakshas, bada, maroon rang, seeng, daant, laal aankhein, bikhre baal, gehri daadhi, bhaari sharir, kaale kapde narangi patka, gehri chamak.
- **Tataka:** ghane jungle mein bahut badi kaali parchhai, bikhre baal, chamakti laal aankhein.
- **Darbari:** sab bade log, alag-alag roop — ganja buzurg mantri safed daadhi (hara vastra), mote kaali daadhi safed pagdi, yuva patla laal pagdi neela vastra, gol-matol peeli pagdi sleti moonchh (maroon vastra), adhed hari pagdi ghani moonchh.
- **Siddhashram ke yagya rishi:** sleti jata wale bhagwa vastra; kaali jata wale safed vastra (mrigchhala, rudraksh, tilak).
- **Yagya ki aag se nikle divya purush:** insaanon se bade, sunehri kirnein, sone ka mukut, sunehri tvacha, lambe kaale baal, laal vastra, haath mein kheer ka sone ka paatra.

## 5. Dikhawat (look)
- Bhag 3 jaisa: maroon-sone ka diamond border; upar title box mein "रामायण · भाग X" + bada sawaal; beech mein scene ki khidki; neeche subtitle box jisme bolne wale ka naam sone ke rang mein.
- Background sajawat se bhare: jhandiyan, gende ki maala, gol mehraab, diamond khambe, nadi ki lehrein, lok-kala wala suraj, rangoli, diye, garm roshni.
- 2D lok-kala storybook style (moti kaali outline, saffron/peela/laal/hara/neela). 3D, asli photo ya anime **nahi**.
- Characters badi resolution mein, taaki close-up saaf rahe. Screen chhoti lage toh characters bade, style wahi.
- Raj-darbar asli jaisa: seedhiyon par oonchi gaddi, upar chhatra, dono taraf baithe darbari.
- Rang se mood: khushi garm-chamakdaar, dukh sleti-neela dheema, gussa laal roshni, dar laal-kaala, ashcharya sunehra. Samay beetne par aasmaan ka rang badle.
- Rakshas pehle kaali parchhai + laal aankhein, phir saamne; naam aate hi mahaul laal-kaala + dhol. Devta sunehri kirnon ke saath.
- "Ghor van" sach mein gehra aur ghana dikhe (bel, ullu, panchhi, kisi jaanwar ki aankhein).

## 6. Camera aur movement (film director ki tarah)
- Har insaan frame mein **ya poora andar ya poora bahar** — aadha kata nahi. Background ki cheezein bhi kinare par aadhi na katein.
- Sir ke upar maathe jitni jagah (mukut/jata upar ke kinare ko na chhuye); aankhein upar ke teesre hisse par; jis taraf character dekh/chal raha ho us taraf zyada jagah.
- Bolne wale par camera zoom. Raftaar scene ke hisaab se (tez scene mein jaldi cut, bhavuk scene mein dheema lamba shot; VIRAL.md section 3); koi khaali second nahi.
- Movement **smooth** (jhatke nahi): chalne mein pair baari-baari uthein (fisalna nahi); khada hona dheere-dheere; mudna/haath uthana beech ke frames se (dhundhla aar-paar nahi).
- Yagya **baith kar** hota hai (aasan par paalthi maar kar, kund ke chaaron taraf).
- Rishi ke aane par raja **kamar se jhuk kar haath jod ke pranam** karta hai; rishi haath utha ke aashirvaad deta hai. Jhukte waqt koi kisi ko chhuye nahi.
- Kahani ki zaroori cheez saaf dikhe (jaise divya purush dhakkan khol ke kheer dikhaye).
- Ram shaant aur nidar — sawaal poochte waqt bhi dara hua nahi.

## 7. Subtitle, chip, awaaz
- Subtitle karaoke: poori line halki, har shabd **theek bole jaane ke time** par chamke, 1–2 khaas shabd peele. Purani line poori hat jaaye tab nayi aaye; naam ki chip bhi.
- Har hisse par 2–3 shabd ki Hindi heading chip; chip ya widget kabhi chehra/pair na dhake; chip cut ke saath hate.
- Mushkil baat (vardaan, shakti) chhote icon ya ginti se (jaise पुत्र: 0 → 4).
- **Awaaz: free Madhur voice** (hi-IN-MadhurNeural). Har character ka alag tone: Ram thoda ooncha, Vishwamitra dheema-gehra. User ko batao kaunsi awaaz kiske liye hai.
- Har line ki awaaz **ek barabar zor** ki; lambe chup ke gap kaat do.
- Jungle ki awaaz + dhol/whoosh/chamak wale effects (free CC0 `sfx/`). Trending gaana video mein nahi — upload ke waqt app mein "Add sound".

## 8. Logo aur Subscribe
- **Katha Chitra logo** chhota, right side, **poore video** mein.
- Aakhri line ke dauraan neeche chhota, aaj ke style ka subscribe widget slide hokar aaye (alag end screen nahi): ungli SUBSCRIBE dabaye ("click" awaaz, button "✓ SUBSCRIBED" bane) → phir ghanti dabaye (chamak + hilna + "ting-ting"). Aakhri frame ting khatam hone tak ruke. Click/ting sirf aakhir mein, kathan ke upar nahi.

## 9. Cover (thumbnail)
- Cover alag se design ho (video ka frame nahi): bada chamakta "?" wala sawaal, title box, Katha Chitra logo, khaas cheezein beech ke 3:4 hisse mein.
- Cover video ke **pehle 0.1 sec** par lagta hai (gallery thumbnail + YouTube cover).

## 10. Director check (bhejne se pehle zaroori)
- Poora video khud dekho: har ¼ sec ka frame, chehre/haath/dhanush/kinare zoom karke.
- Movement lagatar frames mein, awaaz aur subtitle ka mel, awaaz ka level.
- Pichhle version se milao — kuch kharab toh nahi hua.
- Jo kami mile wo turant theek karo, phir dobara jaancho; **tabhi bhejo**.
