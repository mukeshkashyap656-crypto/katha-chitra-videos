## Seekh (AI ne in Shorts se nikali)
* **Sawaal se shuruat:** Viewer ki curiosity badhane ke liye seedha unseen sawaal poocho. *(Example: "What Happened to Mandodari After Ravan Died?")*
* **Chaunkane wale shabdon ka upyog:** Hook mein 'TOO Brutal', 'Actually Say' ya 😱 emoji laga kar chaunkaane wala angle do. *(Example: "Original Ramayana Is TOO Brutal to Adapt🔥")*
* **Chhota shabd sankhya (Length):** Pehli line ya main title ko 5 se 8 shabdon ke andar crisp rakho taaki ek baar mein samajh aaye. *(Example: "Mandodari: What the Ramayana Movie Didn't Tell You!")*
* **Baar-baar aane wale keywords aur Comparison:** 'Valmiki Ramayana', 'Ramayan vs Adipurush' jaise viral keywords ka turant zikr karo. *(Example: "Sita Haran: What Does Valmiki Actually Say?" / "ADIPURUSH vs RAMAYAN")*
* **Raaz aur Hidden Facts:** "Did you know" ya "Unseen story" jaise trigger phrases se an-suni baat ka suspense banao. *(Example: "Did you know that Hanumanji also wrote Ramayan")*
