* **Sawaal se shuruaat:** Direct curious sawaal ("What Happened", "Did you know", "Kaun tha") se viewer ka dhyan khinchein (Example: "What Happened to Mandodari After Ravan Died?").
* **Chaunkane wali baat aur raaz:** "Brutal", "Rahasyamayi", "Didn't Tell You" jaise shabdon aur emoji (😱, 🔥) se raaz banayein (Example: "Original Ramayana Is TOO Brutal to Adapt🔥").
* **Ankda aur comparison:** Numbers, Part, ya "VS" format ka use karke instant comparison dikhayein (Example: "ADIPURUSH vs RAMAYAN | Which Ravan is Better Part 2").
* **Kitne shabd (Length):** Title ko hamesha chhota aur crisp 5 se 10 shabdon ke beech rakhein (Example: "Did Ramayana Just Kill Subtitles?").
* **Baar-baar aane wale shabd:** Title mein "Ramayana", "Valmiki", "Hanuman", "Sita", "Ravan" jaise core keywords zaroor shamil karein (Example: "Pushpaka Vimana: Which Ramayana Is Closest to Valmiki Ramayana?").
