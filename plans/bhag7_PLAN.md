# Bhag 7 — Yagya ki Raksha: Maricha aur Subahu (Plan)
Valmiki Bal Kand Sarg 30. Bhag 6 Siddhashram yagya shuru hone ke teaser par khatam hua tha.

## Kahani (Valmiki se)
- Siddhashram mein 6 din ka yagya shuru hota hai. Ram aur Lakshman 6 din-raat dhanush lekar pahra dete hain.
- 6th din aakash se khoon ki varsha hoti hai. Rakshas Maricha aur Subahu yagya mein vighna dalne aate hain.
- Shri Ram Manavasthra se Maricha ko 100 yojan door samudra mein phenk dete hain.
- Shri Ram Agneyastra se Subahu ka vadh karte hain, aur Lakshman Vayavyastra se baki rakshason ko bhaga dete hain.
- Yagya safal hota hai, Vishwamitra aur rishi Ram-Lakshman ko aashirvaad dete hain.

## Shot List
1. Yagya kund ke chaaron taraf Vishwamitra aur rishi baithe hain (n1)
2. Ram aur Lakshman dhanush liye pahra de rahe hain (n1 at_word: pahra)
3. Aakash kaala, khoon ki varsha, Ram-Lakshman aascharya/firman (n2)
4. Maricha aur Subahu ki kaali parchhai + angry poses (m1)
5. Ram Lakshman ko ishaara karte hue (ram1)
6. Ram Manavasthra chalaate hue, Maricha par prahaar (n3)
7. Ram Agneyastra chalaate hue, Subahu ka vadh (n4)
8. Yagya safal, Vishwamitra aashirvaad dete hue (vm1)
9. Ram aur Vishwamitra aage badhte hue (n5)

## Characters
- Ram, Lakshman, Vishwamitra, Rishi1, Rishi2, Maricha, Subahu (sab pehle se bane hain).
