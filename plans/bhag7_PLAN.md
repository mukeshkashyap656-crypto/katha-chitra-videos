# Bhag 7 — Siddhashram Yagya Raksha (Maricha aur Subahu Vadh)
Valmiki Bal Kand Sarg 30-31.

## Shot list (2-3s per shot)
1. Ashram mein Yagya: Ram aur Lakshman dhanush lekar pahra dete hue, Vishwamitra yagya kund ke paas baithe.
2. Vishwamitra bolte hain ki aaj chheta din sabse zaroori aur saavdhan rehne ka hai.
3. Aakash mein kaale baadal aur yagya kund par laal rakt-varsha (blood rain fx).
4. Maricha ki parchhai + laal aankhen aakash mein (shadow rakshas).
5. Maricha aur Subahu dono aakash mein (lift=200) yagya ko apavitra karne ke liye garajte hain.
6. Ram close-up: Manavastra nikaalte hain aur Lakshman se bolte hain.
7. Ram Manavastra chalate hain (astra fx) — Maricha 100 yojan door samudra mein ja girta hai (result: thrown).
8. Ram Agneyastra chalate hain — Subahu ka vadh hota hai (result: fall).
9. Vishwamitra prashann hokar Ram ko aashirvaad dete hain.
10. Teeno shaant ashram mein khade — agle bhaag ka teaser (Mithila yatra / Ganga katha).
