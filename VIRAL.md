# Katha Chitra — Viral aur Jaanch ki Kitaab

Ye NIYAM.md ke saath padho. NIYAM.md = kya banana hai. Ye file = kaise ki log dekhein, aur kaise jaanche ki video post karne layak hai.
Dhyan: ye ek **pareeksha** hai, pakka formula nahi. Roz ke views `stats/STATS.md` mein likhe jaate hain; har hafte dekho kaun sa tareeka chala aur isi file ko badlo.

## 1. Shuruaat (pehle 2-3 second) — SABSE ZAROORI
**Kyon:** 24 Nov 2026 ka asli aankda: 802 views par sirf 30.2% log ruke, 69.8% ne swipe kar diya; kul dekhna 1.4 ghanta = har view par aausat sirf ~6 second, jabki video 40-60 sec ki hai. Yaani log hook ke baad pehle 6 second mein hi nikal jaate hain. Isi wajah se 0 subscriber (end widget tak koi pahunchta hi nahi). Sabse achha video (472 views) sabse chhota (0:41) tha aur sawaal se shuru hua. Dikkat ek udaharan: Bhag 6 ki pehli line 17 shabd ki thi aur chaunkane wali baat ("tin kadam mein tinon lok") line ke ekdam end mein aayi, tab tak log ja chuke the.
- Pehli line **12 shabd se zyada nahi** (bolne mein ~3 sec). Sabse chaunkane wali baat/sawaal **pehle shabdon mein**, line ke end mein nahi. Peechhe ki baat baad mein.
  - Galat: "Is ashram mein khud Bhagwan Vishnu ne tap kiya tha... aur yahin se unhone tin kadam mein tinon lok naap liye!"
  - Sahi: "Sirf 3 kadam mein tinon lok naap liye? Kisne?" (phir jawab aage kahani mein).
- Pehla jawab 15-20 sec tak mat do: pehli line mein sawaal ya adhoora raaz chhedo (open loop), taaki jawab ke liye log rukein. Video ke beech (~15-20 sec) ek naya chhota mod/sawaal (dobara pakadne ke liye).
- Awaaz **0.0-0.3 sec** se shuru. Koi khaali shuruaat, logo-intro ya dheemi tamheed nahi. (Cover sirf pehle 0.1 sec.)
- Pehle shot mein **1 sec ke andar harkat**: camera dheere zoom, chip ka pop, chamak/mood rang ya character ka action. Pehla cut 2.5 sec se pehle.
- Pehla shot sabse zordaar chitra ho (chehre ka close-up, shock ya bada devta/rakshas), shaant wide shot nahi.
- Ooper ek sawaal ka banner poore video mein tika rahe.
- Pehli frame par cover (0.1 sec).
- Lambai 35-45 sec (55 sec se upar nahi). Lambi video mein swipe zyada hota hai; Bhag 3-5 56-66 sec ki thi.
- Aakhri line mein agle bhaag ka sawaal + "कमेंट में लिखिए: जय श्री राम" + 2-3 shabd "सब्सक्राइब कीजिए" (widget ke saath), aur ye sawaal shuruaat ke sawaal se juda ho (dobara dekhne ki wajah).
- Naap: har episode ke baad YouTube Studio ka "Stayed to watch" (rukne wale %) aur aausat dekhne ka time `stats/STATS.md` mein likho. Lakshya: 30% se upar, phir 50%. Pehle (24 Nov) ka aadhaar 30.2% / ~6 sec.

## 2. Kahani ka dhaancha
hook -> peechhe ki baat -> mod -> raaz -> nateeja -> bhavuk aakhri line -> agle bhaag ka sawaal + "कमेंट करें: जय श्री राम".
Lambai lagbhag 35-45 sec. Koi khaali second nahi. Sirf Valmiki Ramayan, apni taraf se kuch nahi.

## 3. Raftaar: scene ke hisaab se (user ka niyam)
- Tez scene (ladai, khulasa, achanak mod): jaldi-jaldi cut, 1-2 sec ke shot.
- Bhavuk scene (dukh, vidaai, aashirvaad): dheema camera, lamba shot, chehre par tike rehna.
- Jagah ya mahaul dikhana ho (van, nagri, darbaar): chauda shot, aaram se.
- Baatcheet: jo bol raha hai us par cut; bolne wala badle toh cut.
- Ek hi shot itna lamba na ho ki bore lage. Koi khaali second nahi.
- Har scene ki raftaar shot-list mein pehle likho, phir banao.

## 4. Dilchaspi badhane wali cheezein
- Takraav: khalnayak ki shakti banam nayak ka sahas (chhote counter jaise: putra 0 -> 4).
- Shabd-dar-shabd subtitle, khaas shabd peela.
- Har character ki awaaz ka alag andaaz.
- Cut aur khulase par whoosh / dhol / chamak ki awaaz (sfx/ se, CC0).
- Rakshas pehle parchhai + laal aankhein, devta sunehri kirnon ke saath.
- Aakhir mein ek majboot line (seekh ya aaj ki zindagi se jod), phir agle bhaag ka sawaal.

## 5. Post ke niyam
- Title: sawaal + bada naam + emoji + "रामायण भाग X" + "Valmiki Ramayan" + #shorts.
- Tags alag-alag (ek lambi comma wali nahi): valmiki ramayan, ramayana facts, ramayan story. Film/actor ke naam nahi.
- Shaam 7 baje IST, YouTube aur Instagram dono par. Description mein agle bhaag ka sawaal.

## 6. JAANCH KI LIST (PASS / FAIL) — post se pehle
Jaanchne wale AI ko check_sheet (har 0.25 sec ka frame) aur episode.json do. Har cheez ko PASS ya FAIL likho. Sirf wahi galti likho jo sach mein dikh rahi ho.

**Seedhe FAIL (inme se ek bhi ho toh video post nahi hoga):**
1. Koi insaan frame ke kinare se aadha kata hua.
2. Do character ek-doosre ke upar ya aar-paar.
3. Character ka chehra/kapde/rang kisi scene mein badal gaye.
4. Ram/Lakshman/Dasharatha ke sir ke peechhe halo.
5. Khaali ya kaala frame, ya beech mein ruka hua video.
6. Subtitle ya chip kisi ka chehra dhak raha hai.
7. Katha Chitra logo gayab, ya aakhir mein subscribe widget gayab.
8. Awaaz aur subtitle ka mel nahi, ya kisi line ki awaaz baaki se bahut dheemi.
9. Kahani Valmiki se alag, ya koi cheez apni taraf se jodi gayi.
10. Pehli frame par cover nahi.
11. Pehli line 12 shabd se lambi, ya chaunkane wali baat/sawaal pehle 3 sec mein nahi aaya (section 1). Pehle shot mein 1 sec ke andar koi harkat nahi.
12. Video 55 sec se lambi.

**Number (har ek 0-10, sab milakar 70 mein se kam se kam 56 chahiye):**
- Shuruaat ka hook, kahani ka dhaancha, raftaar scene ke hisaab se, chehre ke bhaav, mood ka rang, aakhri line + agla sawaal, camera ke niyam (headroom, kinare).

**Faisla:** koi seedha FAIL nahi AUR number 56+ ho -> PASS. Warna FAIL.
FAIL par: AI har galti ke liye exact sudhaar likhe, episode.json theek ho, dobara banao (3 koshish tak). Tab bhi FAIL -> post NAHI hoga, user ko issue mein khabar jaaye.

## 7. Seekh ka loop
Har video ke views, reach, likes, comments, shares `stats/STATS.md` mein roz judte hain. Har hafte: top aur neeche ke bhaag dekho (hook ka tarah, sawaal, shuruaat, posting time) aur section 1-4 mein jo seekha wo likho. Sirf ek-do video ke aadhaar par pakka nahi bolna.

## 8. Dusre Ramayan Shorts se seekha (24 Nov 2026) — pareeksha, pakka nahi

Credit wapas aate hi `vidiq_outliers` (keyword "रामायण", contentType short, language hi) se asli views ke saath dobara jaancho.
- **"Asli Valmiki mein kya likha hai" wala dhaancha baar-baar dikha**: "लक्ष्मण रेखा असली रामायण में है ही नहीं!", "What Valmiki actually wrote about Sita's abduction", "Why is Valmiki's Ramayana so brutal?", "Ram never told Sita to walk into the fire", "Valmiki's first verse was a curse...".
